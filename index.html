Why OptiLink Mobility exists

Autonomy solved the driver. It didn't solve the operation — charging, cleaning, staging, insurance, compliance, and putting the right car on the right corner at 5pm on a Friday.

That's OptiLink Mobility. We own and operate Cybercab fleets on Tesla's network across Dallas–Fort Worth, so every ride shows up charged, spotless, and on time. Tesla provides the autonomy. We provide the standard.

Summon. Board. Arrive.

How it works
01

Request in the Tesla app

Open the Tesla Robotaxi app and set your destination. A OptiLink Mobility-operated Cybercab is dispatched from the nearest staging depot — typically minutes away.

02

Step into your Cybercab

Two seats, no wheel, no pedals. Your phone unlocks the doors. Climate is already set. The cabin is inspected and detailed between every shift.

03

The city glides past

Unsupervised full self-driving, monitored 24/7 by OptiLink Mobility fleet operations. Fixed upfront pricing. Step out and walk away — payment is automatic.

The fleet

Always staged, never stale

Depot-based operations keep cars charged, detailed, and positioned where demand actually happens — airports at dawn, stadiums at nine, campuses at five.

Every vehicle is monitored by OptiLink Mobility fleet operations around the clock, with first-responder protocols filed with the State of Texas. Autonomy with accountability.
